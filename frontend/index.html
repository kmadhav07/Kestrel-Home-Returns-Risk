    Architectural & Policy Decisions
    
      
    
        
    
          1. Why Confirmation Calls Beat Blanket Holds:
          
    
            Holding orders for >24 hours triggers a 12% customer cancellation on legitimate non-returning orders, destroying ₹2,400+ in gross margin per cancellation. At ₹45 per call preventing 35% of returns, pre-dispatch calls yield an 11.18% break-even threshold and net +₹1.41 Lakhs annual profit.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          2. Target Leakage Exclusions:
          
    
            `pickup_scheduled_at` and post-delivery events (`REVERSE_PICKUP`, `DEMO_DONE`) only exist after returns are initiated. We strictly excluded them from features.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          3. Reconstructed Basket Value:
          
    
            The October 2025 payment gateway stored paise as rupees (100x scaling anomaly). Reconstructing `list_price * qty * (1 - discount_pct/100)` repairs this error across all time periods.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          4. Shield Member Relationship Protection:
          
    
            Shield members return more often because policy grants free 30-day returns. They also buy 3+ appliances/year. Our rule explicitly forbids automatic holds on Shield members.
